Architecture illustration · Every warning has a trail.

The problem

A borrower can deteriorate between financial reporting dates. Payment behavior, account activity, and industry signals may reveal the change before the next statement arrives.

A risk score alone is not enough for a credit decision. A reviewer needs to see the calculation, the source, the threshold, and the evidence.

The build

Covenant Radar forecasts potential commercial loan covenant breaches at 30, 60, and 90 days. A six-step nightly pipeline ingests data, tests covenants, scores risk, ranks warnings, updates cases, and dispatches results.

Financial statements are normalized into a common chart of accounts. Invalid inputs enter a quarantine queue. Contractual ratios are checked against the threshold in force for the relevant period.

The decision

Keep contractual mathematics separate from behavioral risk. The covenant calculation stays deterministic and inspectable. External and behavioral signals contribute a separately identified layer.

Each warning can be reconstructed from stored source data, threshold versions, calculations, evidence, and review history.

Guarded by design

A single guarded LLM call site applies input masking, provider checks, token budgets, and prompt-hash versioning. Attempts and refusals are recorded.

Eight-role access control, maker-checker approvals, a hash-chained audit trail, and an evaluation harness define who can act and what must pass before release.

Scope & evidence

The repository describes a demo book of 24 NSE-listed Indian companies, using their filed quarterly results, with a public-data workspace for news and economic context. It is a demonstrator, not a claim of deployment at a lending institution.
